Bat Family

The Boy Wonder: Who is Robin?
He is the colorful contrast to the Dark Knight’s shadow. Introduced in 1940, Robin is not just Batman’s sidekick—he is the heart of Gotham City's fight against crime. While Batman represents vengeance and fear, Robin brings hope, energy, and a vital emotional anchor.

Dick Grayson (The Original): A brilliant circus acrobat who lost his parents to tragedy, much like Bruce Wayne. He later becomes Nightwing.

Skills and Abilities:
- Master martial artist
- Incredible acrobat
- Uses advanced gadgets
- Highly intelligent strategist

The Bright Knight: Who is Batgirl?
Fierce, brilliant, and independent, Batgirl is one of Gotham’s strongest heroes. She earns her role through intelligence and determination.

Skills:
- Genius-level intellect
- Martial arts expert
- Master hacker
- Uses high-tech gear

The Outlaw of Gotham: Who is Red Hood?
Red Hood is the darker version of a hero. He believes crime should be stopped permanently.

Jason Todd: A former Robin who returns stronger and more dangerous.

Skills:
- Expert fighter
- Master with weapons
- Tactical strategist
- Enhanced abilities from Lazarus Pit

The Brightest Star: Who is Nightwing?
Nightwing is the original Robin grown into his own hero. He represents leadership and hope.

Skills:
- Best acrobat in DC
- Martial arts master
- Uses Escrima sticks
- Natural leader
